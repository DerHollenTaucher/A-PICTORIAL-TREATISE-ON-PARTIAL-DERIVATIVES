设 z = f(x, y) 为一曲面。把 y 冻结在 y₀，以平面 y = y₀ 切下，曲面上便留出一条曲线 Cx；沿此曲线行走，高度只随 x 一个变量改变。这条曲线在 P 点的切线斜率，即 f 对 x 的偏导数。同理冻结 x，得 Cy 与 ∂f/∂y。几何上，偏导数就是沿坐标轴方向的坡度：在图上恰是那个斜率三角的高与底之比。

Let z = f(x, y) be a surface. Freeze y at y₀ and cut with the plane y = y₀: the surface retains a curve Cx along which the height varies with x alone. The slope of its tangent at P is the partial derivative of f with respect to x. Freezing x in like manner gives Cy and ∂f/∂y. Geometrically a partial derivative is a gradient measured along one coordinate axis — precisely the rise over the run of the little slope triangle drawn upon the curve.

∂f∂x (x₀, y₀) = limh→0 f(x₀+h, y₀) − f(x₀, y₀)h

注在马鞍面上，两个方向于原点处的坡度反号：一个上坡，一个下坡。这正是偏导数只看得见“一条线”的证据——离开这条线，它一无所知。

NoteOn a saddle the two slopes at the origin have opposite signs: one climbs, one falls. This is the witness that a partial derivative sees only a single line — away from that line it knows nothing.

试一试 —— 拖动舞台上的红色图钉，看斜率三角如何翻转符号；再把 a 与 b 调得不相等，两方向便不再对称。

Try this — drag the red pin across the stage and watch the slope triangle change sign; then set a and b unequal and the two directions lose their symmetry.

把两个偏导数并排收入一个向量，便得梯度 ∇f = (∂f/∂x, ∂f/∂y)。在等高线图上，梯度恒垂直于过该点的等值线，并指向函数增长最速的一方；其长度 |∇f| 就是那一方的上升率。两个偏导数，不过是梯度在两根坐标轴上的投影。

Gather the two partial derivatives into one vector and you have the gradient, ∇f = (∂f/∂x, ∂f/∂y). Upon a contour map the gradient stands always perpendicular to the level curve through the point, and points the way of steepest ascent; its length |∇f| is the rate of that ascent. The two partial derivatives are merely its projections upon the axes.

∇f ⟂ { f = const } ,   |∇f| = max|u|=1 Duf

注把参数 a 调到零，马鞍面退化为一片平原，等高线由双曲线族骤然摊成平行直线——退化过程本身即是一课。

NoteDrive the parameter a to zero and the saddle collapses into a plain; the hyperbolas of the level sets flatten abruptly into parallel lines. The degeneracy is itself the lesson.

试一试 —— 拖动舞台，从正上方俯视等高线；再让取点沿着一条等值线滑动，箭头始终与之垂直。

Try this — orbit the stage to look straight down upon the contours; then slide the point along one level curve and see the arrow keep its right angle.

若不定沿坐标轴行走，而以单位向量 u = (cos θ, sin θ) 为方向，过 P 作含 u 的竖直平面，同样切出一条曲线；其切线斜率即方向导数。它对 u 是线性的：只要记住两个偏导数，任意方向的坡率便可算出。

Should we walk not along an axis but along a unit vector u = (cos θ, sin θ), a vertical plane through P containing u cuts yet another curve, and the slope of its tangent is the directional derivative. It is linear in u: knowing the two partials, the slope in any direction whatever may be computed.

Duf = ∇f · u = fx cos θ + fy sin θ

注作为 θ 的函数，这是一条余弦曲线；若以极坐标作图，便是一个过原点的圆——图版中那朵“玫瑰”。半径最长处正是 ∇f 的方向；与 ∇f 垂直时半径为零，即沿等高线行走，高度不变。

NoteAs a function of θ this is a cosine; drawn in polar coordinates it is a circle through the origin — the “rose” upon the plate. The longest radius falls upon the direction of ∇f; where u is perpendicular to it the radius vanishes, for walking along a level curve changes no height.

试一试 —— 拨动角度指针，让玫瑰图上的半径伸到最长，再转九十度，看它缩为零。

Try this — turn the needle until the radius of the rose is longest, then turn a further ninety degrees and watch it fall to nothing.

若 f 在 P 处可微，则曲面在该点附近可由一张切平面逼近。切平面给出的高度变化 dz = fx dx + fy dy 称为全微分；真实的变化 Δz 与之相差一个比 √(dx²+dy²) 更高阶的小量。两个偏导数，正是这一线性逼近的两个系数——这便是它们在工程近似中无处不在的缘故。

If f be differentiable at P, the surface near that point is approximated by a tangent plane. The change of height it predicts, dz = fx dx + fy dy, is called the total differential; the true change Δz differs from it by a quantity of higher order than √(dx²+dy²). The two partial derivatives are the coefficients of this linear approximation — whence their ubiquity in every art of approximation.

z = f(P) + fx(x−x₀) + fy(y−y₀)  ;  Δz − dz = o(‖Δp‖)

注切平面与曲面之间的缝隙，就是“可微”一词的度量：缝隙相对于步长趋于零，切平面才配称为逼近。

NoteThe gap between plane and surface is the very measure of differentiability: only when the gap shrinks faster than the step may the plane be called an approximation.

试一试 —— 拖动 (Δx, Δy) 手柄，把步长一点点收小，看读数栏里的误差比率如何趋于零。

Try this — drag the (Δx, Δy) handle and shrink the step by degrees; watch the error ratio in the ledger fall away to nothing.
